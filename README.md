# LexBridge Counsel

Production-style demo for an international legal research and chamber workflow app.

## Live Demo

After GitHub Pages is enabled:

```text
https://graphictech360.github.io/lexbridge-counsel/#research
```

## Features

- Jurisdiction-scoped AI legal research demo
- Bangladesh, England & Wales, and United States law sections
- 50 curated case studies
- 47 statute, dhara, and procedural rule references
- Lawyer notes
- Bookmarks
- Chamber management
- Hearing reminders
- Citation export
- Legal drafting
- Mobile installable PWA structure

## Disclaimer

LexBridge Counsel is a workflow and research demo for lawyers and supervised legal teams. It is not legal advice, does not create a lawyer-client relationship, and all outputs require qualified lawyer review against current primary sources and local procedure.
